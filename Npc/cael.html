Cael

Wandering Bard

Overview

An old, perpetually drunken bard seen wandering across Ellond. Cael turns up in towns, villages, taverns and roadside camps with little explanation for how he got there. He tells long, nonsensical stories that rarely seem to have a point, though he delivers every one of them with the confidence of a man recounting important history.

Personality

  • Frequently drunk
  • Talkative once a story begins
  • Cheerful, rambling and difficult to follow
  • Treats improbable nonsense as established fact

Traits

  • Appears in settlements all across Ellond.
  • Seems to know an endless number of stories, songs and questionable legends.
  • Rarely tells the same story exactly the same way twice.

First Impression

Most people first notice the smell of drink, the battered instrument and the voice already halfway through a story. Within minutes, Cael is usually describing some impossible event as though everyone present ought to remember it.

Quote

"I was there, you know. Or near there. Might have been somewhere else entirely. Anyway, that's not the important part."

Wandering

Cael has no known permanent home. He is encountered all over Ellond, usually in taverns, on roads, near markets or wherever someone is willing to listen to a story.
