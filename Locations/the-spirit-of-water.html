The Spirit of Water

The Mistmarch · DM Hidden

Anchor: The Wellspring Region: The Shimmering Lake At the deepest point of the Shimmering Lake lies an ancient freshwater spring called the Wellspring in old tales. The Water Spirit rests there, and its presence keeps the lake exceptionally clean, clear and pleasant to drink. The Spirit can travel through water, but the Wellspring is where its consciousness gathers. The remarkable purity of the lake is commonly treated as a natural wonder in 451, though old stories say a Guardian sleeps beneath its surface. Living Traditions & Superstitions The First Pour. Before a ship leaves port, a sailor pours a small measure of whisky or other strong drink onto the forward deck and says, “One for the water, one for the weather. Carry us out, and carry us home.” The drink must be allowed to wash away naturally; wiping it up is considered poor luck. Water for the Road. Before a long journey, some travellers pour the first mouthful from a newly filled waterskin onto the ground or into the river it came from. Older people say, “Never take the first drink.” Few remember that it was once an acknowledgement of the Water Guardian.
