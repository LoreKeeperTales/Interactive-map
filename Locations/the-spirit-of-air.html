The Spirit of Air

Ellond · DM Hidden

Anchor: The Eye of Heaven Region: The high mountains of Ellond The Air Spirit's Anchor is not buried in the earth. Above a remote high peak lies a permanent convergence of winds known in fragments of old folklore as the Eye of Heaven. At its center is an impossible pocket of still air. Storms and powerful winds can surround it while the center remains silent and motionless. The Spirit may travel within winds and storms, but returns to this place to rest. Since the skies offer little shelter, the Air Spirit is also associated with storms. Old stories often describe the Guardian arriving with thunder rather than appearing beneath a clear sky. Living Traditions & Superstitions The Loose Ribbon. Sailors, riders and travellers crossing exposed high ground sometimes tie a short scrap of cloth or ribbon somewhere the wind can take it. If it eventually tears free, it belongs to the wind. Deliberately retrieving it is considered unlucky. Don't Curse the Wind. Among sailors and travellers it is considered poor practice to curse an opposing wind aloud. Instead they say, “It has somewhere else to be.” The phrase survives even among people who openly mock the old Guardian stories.
