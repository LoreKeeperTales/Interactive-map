Xal'Torath

Lizardfolk City — West Marches

The largest city in the West Marches and the political heart of the Lizardfolk clans.

Population

Approximately 14,000

The City

Xal'Torath is built upon massive stone terraces rising above the surrounding marshes. Bridges, walkways and canals connect the different levels of the city, often winding around enormous mangrove roots.

Much of its oldest stonework predates the Empire by thousands of years.

Government

Xal'Torath is the political heart of the Lizardfolk clans of the West Marches. Clan elders gather here to settle disputes, discuss matters affecting the marshes and represent their people.

Known For

The Marsh Council chambers, Temple of Asgorath, Great Beast Market, exotic spices, dinosaur mounts, ancient stone observatories and the Dragon Relic Museum.

Atmosphere

Ancient stone terraces stand among thick marsh vegetation and enormous roots. Narrow bridges and elevated walkways cross canals and flooded ground, while markets occupy the broader terraces.

The city feels considerably older than most settlements in modern Ellond, with newer homes, markets and workshops built directly among structures whose original purpose has long been forgotten.

History

Xal'Torath grew around ancient stone terraces that had already stood in the marshes for thousands of years. Generations of Lizardfolk clans settled among the ruins, repairing bridges, reopening waterways and adapting the old structures to their own needs.

As the clans of the West Marches became more organized, Xal'Torath developed into their natural meeting place. Its central position and ancient gathering grounds eventually made it the political heart of the Lizardfolk people.

Even as trade and contact with the rest of Ellond increased, Xal'Torath remained strongly tied to the traditions of the marsh clans.
