The Spirit of Earth

Central Highlands · DM Hidden

Anchor: The Worldstone Region: A mountain region of Ellond The Earth Spirit rests around a colossal formation deep beneath the mountains: the Worldstone. It is not a single gem but a mass of ancient stone, crystal and metal saturated with primordial magic. Near the Anchor, geology behaves strangely. Mineral seams can return after being worked, cracks slowly close, and new crystals appear in stone previously thought barren. Old mining stories speak of places where “the mountain grows back.” The precise resting place has been lost from common knowledge by 451. Miners are far more likely to know the superstitions than the truth behind them. Living Traditions & Superstitions The First Strike. Before beginning a new mine face, quarry or tunnel, the first blow is deliberately made lightly. The worker then waits a moment before striking again. The old explanation is simple: “Knock before entering.” The Keeper's Share. Miners sometimes leave one attractive piece of ore or crystal in the wall when finishing a productive seam. Taking every visible piece is called “scraping the Keeper's plate” and is believed to invite collapses or barren rock.
