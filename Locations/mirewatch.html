Mirewatch

Capital City — The Mistmarch

An old fortified city built where the firmer high ground rises above Mistmarch’s wetlands. Mirewatch began as a defensive settlement guarding the few reliable roads through the marshes and gradually became the administrative centre of the region.

History

The oldest parts of Mirewatch stand on defensible high ground above the wetlands. Stone foundations and raised streets were built to survive the wet ground and seasonal flooding, while watchtowers guarded the limited reliable approaches through the marsh. As the settlement became the political centre of Mistmarch, newer districts spread beyond the original walls.
