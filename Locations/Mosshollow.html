Mosshollow

Fishing Village

A quiet fishing village built on stilts above the marsh waters. Wooden walkways connect homes, docks and communal buildings across the wetlands.

Population

Approximately 900

Exports

Salted fish, pearl diving, marsh reeds and river oysters.

Known For

Giant lily fields, a friendly Tortle population and excellent seafood.
