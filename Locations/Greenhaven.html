Greenhaven

Human Capital — Southern Empire

Greenhaven is a prosperous farming and harbour town surrounded by some of the most fertile land in the Southern Empire.

Agriculture

Vast fields, orchards, grazing lands, farmsteads, mills and agricultural villages surround the town. Wheat, barley, oats, vegetables and fruit are produced here in enormous quantities.

Known For

The Grand Market, extensive farmland, grain production and its reputation as the Granary of the South.

History

Greenhaven began as a frontier settlement near the southern edge of the Empire, founded to support trade, farming and travel between the settled lands and the wilder regions beyond.

Over time, merchants, hunters, farmers and travelers turned the settlement into a steady stopping point rather than a major city. Its position made it valuable without ever making it crowded.

Today, Greenhaven is known as a practical and welcoming town where caravans resupply, travelers rest and much of the Southern Empire's food passes through its markets.
