Greenhaven

Major Trade City — Southern Empire

Greenhaven is a prosperous harbour city of approximately 15,000 permanent inhabitants. Its position on the northern edge of the Southern Empire makes it an important meeting point for merchants, sailors, farmers, caravans and travellers from across Ellond.

Population

Approximately 15,000 permanent inhabitants, with the population frequently swelling as traders, sailors and travellers pass through the city.

Known For

The Grand Market, busy docks, regional trade, surrounding farmland and the constant movement of goods between the Southern Empire and the rest of Ellond.
