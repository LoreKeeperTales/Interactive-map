The Spirit of the Forest

Enchanted Forest · DM Hidden

Anchor: The Heartroot Region: The Enchanted Forest The Forest Spirit rests within the living heart of the Enchanted Forest. Beneath its center, roots from ancient trees intertwine into a vast living network known in the oldest stories as the Heartroot. The Spirit is a major reason the Enchanted Forest flourishes so unnaturally. Plants grow with unusual vigor, magic clings to the land, and the forest seems capable of healing or changing itself. The Spirit also guards those it accepts as inhabitants: outcasts, exiles and others who have found sanctuary beneath its canopy. Those entering with ill intent may never reach the forest's center. Paths bend, landmarks move and distances cease to behave properly. Some trespassers never escape. Others eventually emerge hollow and confused, unable to give a coherent account of what happened. Living Traditions & Superstitions The Green Greeting. Before entering an old forest, travellers touch the nearest living tree with an open palm and say, “I enter beneath your branches and leave them as I found them.” Woodcutters, hunters and even soldiers commonly do this. Many insist it is merely good manners. The Last Seed. When gathering food, herbs or timber from a forest, an old custom is to leave the last usable seed, fruit or cutting behind rather than taking everything. The saying is, “Leave something for the Keeper.”
