Port Aster

Port Town — West Marches

Welcome to Port Aster, the largest harbor on the western coast of Ellond and the final stop before venturing into the untamed West Marches. Built where the Emerald River meets the sea, the city is home to merchants, explorers, sailors, and adventurers from every corner of the world.

Known For

Main Exports: exotic spices, rare medicinal herbs, hardwoods, pearls, salted fish, monster materials, tropical fruits, and handcrafted goods from the peoples of the West Marches.
