Elarion

Capital City — Eastern Verdant Kingdoms

Overview

Elarion is the capital of the Eastern Verdant Kingdoms, built where the old eastern forests open into a broad green valley. The city is shaped by cultivated woodland rather than untouched wilderness: timber halls, pale stone, gardens, orchards and elevated walkways sit among ancient trees deliberately preserved within the city. Roads, farms and smaller settlements radiate outward from Elarion, making it both the political heart of the kingdom and a major centre of eastern trade.

Population

Approximately 22,000

Primary: Wood Elves
Secondary: Humans, Gnomes and High Elves

Known For

Forestry, herbalism, fine woodworking, cultivated orchards, hunting, bowmaking and eastern trade.

Government

Seat of the Verdant Crown.

The royal court is centred on Verdant Keep, a pale-stone fortress surrounded by gardens and preserved woodland.

Gentleman's League

Brom Tallow serves as the Regional Royal Mage of the Eastern Verdant Kingdoms and as the highest-ranking Gentleman's League authority in the region.

History

Elarion grew from an older woodland seat into the principal city of the Eastern Verdant Kingdoms. Its rulers expanded around the forest rather than clearing it entirely, creating a capital where government, trade and managed woodland exist side by side.
